CladlyGo – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026  |  FUZIONATIC PRIVATE LIMITED

Welcome to CladlyGo, the app for sharing your wardrobe with people you trust. This Privacy Policy explains how FUZIONATIC PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the CladlyGo mobile app. By using CladlyGo, you agree to this policy.

DPDP Act (India) GDPR CCPA / CPRA COPPA

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email (or Apple private relay email), and a unique account identifier. If you sign up with email, your password is handled securely by Firebase Authentication. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile details: profile photo, phone number, gender, and date of birth.
  • Wardrobe and posts: photos of your clothes, photos and short videos you post (including any sound recorded with a video), captions, item names and categories, and whether an item is open for borrowing or for sale.
  • Activity with others: your circle connections and requests, boutiques you follow, likes, comments, and borrow or buy requests and their status (pending, accepted, declined).
  • Daily Trivia: your answers and scores, which appear on your circle's leaderboard.
  • Support messages: anything you send us through Contact Us or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location (only if you allow it): read only while you are using the app, never in the background. See Section 5.
  • Device and usage information: device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone, and how you use app features (through Firebase Analytics, which you can turn off — see Section 9).
  • Push notification token: used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Logs: technical and error logs used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not collect face data, voice recordings for analysis, contacts from your address book, or payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Show your wardrobe, posts, and profile to the people you choose.
  • Let people find you, add you to their circle, and send borrow or buy requests.
  • Show the Nearby feed and tag public posts with an approximate area.
  • Send notifications about requests, comments, likes, and circle activity.
  • Run Daily Trivia and leaderboards.
  • Keep the community safe: filter offensive text, review reports, and act on accounts that break our Terms.
  • Understand how the app is used so we can fix bugs and improve features.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Who Can See What You Share

  • Your circle: posts are shared with your circle by default. People in your circle can see your profile, your posts, and items you mark as open for borrowing or for sale.
  • Public posts: if you choose to share a post publicly, people using the Nearby feed within about 5 km can see it, along with its approximate (about 1 km) area.
  • Finding you: other users can find you by searching your name, exact email address, or phone number so they can add you to their circle.
  • Boutiques: boutique profiles and their posts are visible to users who view or follow them.
  • Comments, likes, and requests: visible to the people involved in that post or request.

Images and videos you post are delivered through a content delivery network using links that are hard to guess, but anyone who has a link can view that file. Please don't post anything you wouldn't want shared.

4. Borrowing, Lending, and Buying

CladlyGo lets users send borrow and buy requests to each other. We do not process payments, hold deposits, or take part in any exchange. Any payment, handover, or return is arranged directly between the users involved. Contact details you share with another user (for example through WhatsApp) are governed by that service's privacy policy.

5. Location Data

  • Location is optional and used only when you allow it ("While Using the App"). We never track you in the background.
  • Your position is rounded to about 1 km. The rounded location is stored only with posts you choose to share publicly, so they can appear in the Nearby feed (5 km radius).
  • If you search for a place by name, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to find it.
  • You can turn location off at any time in your device Settings. The rest of the app keeps working; the Nearby feed and location tags will be unavailable.

6. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: to photograph clothes and record videos for posts.
  • Photo Library: to choose photos and videos to post, and optionally to save photos you capture.
  • Microphone: only to record sound with videos you choose to record.
  • Location (approximate, while using the app): for the Nearby feed and location tags on public posts.
  • Notifications: for requests, comments, likes, and circle activity.

We only access these when you use the related feature, and only with your permission.

7. Community Safety and Moderation

Comments, captions, and other text you post are checked against a list of offensive terms on our own servers before they are published. We do not send this text to outside AI services for moderation. You can report posts and block users in the app. We review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts that break our Terms of Service.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud Platform: servers that process uploads, requests, and moderation, plus logging. Google Cloud Privacy Notice
  • Cloudflare (R2 and CDN): storage and fast delivery of images and videos. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Apple and Google sign-in: Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. You can revoke access at any time via Google Account Settings or your Apple ID settings.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): place search, only when you search for a location. OSMF Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp: opens only when you tap a WhatsApp button; we do not share data with WhatsApp ourselves.

9. Analytics and Tracking

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and where the app needs improvement. Analytics is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not use advertising identifiers or cookies. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings in the app.

10. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send notifications about requests, comments, likes, and circle activity. FCM receives your device token, device model, OS version, language, time zone, and IP address to deliver them. You can turn notifications off in your device Settings at any time.

11. Data Retention

  • Account and profile details: until you delete your account.
  • Wardrobe photos, posts, and videos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Comments, likes, requests, and circle connections: until deleted by you or removed when your account is deleted.
  • Trivia scores: until your account is deleted.
  • Location on public posts: until the post is deleted.
  • Logs: up to 90 days, then automatically deleted.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, wardrobe items, and associated files from our systems within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations.

12. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including:

  1. Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  2. Encrypted storage for sensitive data kept on your device.
  3. Database and storage access restricted by security rules and access controls.
  4. Secure authentication through Firebase Authentication, Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In.
  5. Regular review and updates of our security measures.

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure.

13. Your Choices

  • Edit your profile and delete individual posts and wardrobe items in the app.
  • Delete your account and data at any time from Settings.
  • Turn off location, camera, photos, microphone, and notifications in your device Settings.
  • Turn off analytics in Settings in the app.
  • Block users and report content.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live — including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK, and the CCPA/CPRA in California — you may have the right to:

  1. Access: request a copy of your personal data.
  2. Correction: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  3. Erasure: request deletion of your data.
  4. Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  5. Restrict or object: limit or object to certain processing.
  6. Withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  7. Grievance redressal and complaints: contact our Grievance Officer, or complain to your data protection authority.
  8. Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

To make a request, email support@fuzionatic.ai.

15. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on:

  1. Your consent (for example, location and analytics).
  2. Contractual necessity to provide the service you signed up for.
  3. Our legitimate interests in keeping the app safe and improving it.
  4. Compliance with legal obligations.

16. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Categories of personal information collected in the past 12 months:

  • Identifiers (name, email, phone number, account identifiers)
  • Personal characteristics you provide (gender, date of birth)
  • Approximate location
  • Device and usage information
  • User-generated content (wardrobe photos, posts, videos, comments, requests)

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Response timeline for CCPA requests: 45 days.

17. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process your data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

18. Children's Privacy

CladlyGo is not intended for children under 13, and users must meet the minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

19. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will:

  1. Notify affected users without undue delay.
  2. Report to the relevant authorities within the time required by law (for example, 72 hours under the GDPR).
  3. Describe the nature of the breach, the types of data affected, and the steps we are taking.

20. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email, and update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
